Every card you've saved, in one key you hold.

When you check out with a card at a Braintree or PayPal merchant, that merchant keeps its own saved copy. You can't see them all, and you can't take one back. Keyring shows every card you've saved across merchants, lets you lock or revoke any one (you confirm each action yourself), and explains any charge you don't recognize.

1 · Your key

A random secret only you hold. Merchants link saved cards to its public handle, not to your email or phone number.

2 · Merchants (demo checkouts)

Each merchant saves your card in its own PayPal vault (real sandbox calls: setup token, then payment token) and charges it with Orders v2.

3 · Your Keyring

Every saved card across merchants. Lock blocks new charges (reversible). Revoke deletes the saved card from that merchant's PayPal vault.
